Open blockers

Tracked in known-issues.md, updated as the build progresses
Resolved

RAMS principal eligibility — was blocked on a ComplianceProvider mismatch from a Brickken Sandbox redeployment; fixed by Brickken on Sep 10. Mandate and operator approval both confirmed on-chain the same day.

Brickken side

Test USDT funding — the sandbox payment token's mint() is access-controlled; the issuer wallet holds 0 USDT until Brickken funds it, which blocks an actual value-moving execute.

Our side

Ops & Compliance gas — both wallets still need a little Sepolia ETH before they can sign their own steps (execute and revokeMandate aren't relayable through the issuer).
