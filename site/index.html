The whole point of this build

Six moves. The permission gets tested, taken away, and then it pays for real.

Every step below happened in this order, on the real chain, in one working session — not staged, not simulated. The Ops Agent's authority is granted, tested, refused when it oversteps, cut off by a different agent entirely, confirmed gone, then proven to move real money once it exists.

How

The mechanism behind the sequence

RAMS (ERC‑8226) separates two things most systems conflate: who is allowed to act, and who is allowed to stop them. The Issuer grants the first to Ops. It grants the second, separately, to Compliance.
